# The traces the Metal AV1 encoder's learned pruning trains and is tested on (scripts/train-pruner;
# BENCHMARKS "learned pruning of the analysis"). One trace a line:
#
#   <file in ~/Library/Application Support/Glass Act/traces>  <train frames>  <test frames>  <name>
#
# Train frames are a range, first-last inclusive (`train-pruner dump` takes 5 pictures spread over
# it), or a comma list of frames. Test frames are a range: `train-pruner compare` codes its first
# frames (key frames: 10 of them; streams: up to 100). The two mustn't overlap. A trace without
# its file is skipped with a warning, so this list can name traces only some Macs have.

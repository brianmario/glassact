## libaom (Alliance for Open Media)

Parts of `av1f/` reproduce libaom v3.13.1 encoder decisions so that `--tune iq`
matches `avifenc`/libaom's all-intra tune=iq behaviour: the tune=iq frame
decisions and variance boost (`av1f/include/av1f/tuneiq.h`,
`av1f/src/varboost.cu`), the loop-filter and CDEF strength searches
(`av1f/src/lfsearch.cu`), and the quantization-matrix weight tables
(`av1f/include/av1f/aom_tables_gen.h`, generated by `tools/gen_aom_tables.py`
from `av1/common/quant_common.c`). The range encoder in
`av1core/include/av1g/entenc.h` is a port of `aom_dsp/entenc.c`.

## AV1 bitstream specification

The tables in `av1core/include/av1g/{spec_tables,cdf_defaults,enums_gen,levels_gen}.h`
are generated by `tools/gen_tables.py` from the AV1 Bitstream & Decoding Process
Specification (<https://github.com/AOMediaCodec/av1-spec>), published by the
Alliance for Open Media.
